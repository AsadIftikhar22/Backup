Salam’s Strategy

The Group has developed the NEXT Strategy for the years 2025 to 2029 which aims to solidify its position as one of the leading entities in the Kingdom’s digital transformation landscape. The strategy encompasses several pillars that integrate core expansion, product innovation, the Saudi Experience initiative, a transformational journey, and the positioning of Technical Links Services Company as an independent infrastructure provider.

New Products and Services
The Group prioritizes innovation to address emerging trends and strengthen its competitive position. It is expanding its portfolio with digital financial services, cloud gaming, smart home security, IoT solutions, private 5G networks for sectors such as healthcare and logistics, and enhanced satellite connectivity to support digital inclusion.
Leveraging its connectivity footprint, the Group is enabling sector focused AI and edge solutions for low latency applications across smart cities, mobility, healthcare, customer experience, and oil and gas. These initiatives, including edge computing and UCaaS platforms, enhance real time capabilities and reinforce the Group’s value proposition and market leadership. .
Read More
Core Expansion
The Group is strengthening its network infrastructure to meet growing demand, with a focus on expanding its fiber network, improving last mile connectivity, upgrading metro and wide area networks, and enhancing cross border capacity, including a 600 kilometre link to Oman.
In parallel, Salam is expanding its B2C and B2B offerings by targeting SMEs and large enterprises with advanced cloud, ICT, and managed services. These efforts position the Group as an integrated ICT partner, simplifying client operations while supporting growth.
The strategy aligns with Vision 2030 and emphasizes emerging technologies such as cloud, AI, machine learning, data analytics, and robotics to drive innovation and customer value.
Read More
Saudi Experience
The Group is reinforcing its identity as a distinctly Saudi brand by strengthening its market positioning, promoting environmental sustainability, and supporting local partnerships. Through green initiatives and eco friendly practices, it underscores its commitment to sustainable development. At the same time, the Group is enhancing customer engagement by digitizing customer care, leveraging advanced analytics, and tailoring offerings to key segments such as youth, family gamers, and expatriates, aligning innovation with local values.

Transformational Journey
The Group focuses on operational excellence, digital efficiency, and sustainability in line with Vision 2030. It adopts a digital operating model supported by advanced analytics and cloud systems to enhance agility and decision making, while improving inventory management and last mile services to optimize resources. In parallel, the Group advances green telecom initiatives through energy efficient network and data center practices, certifications, and partnerships that support long term value creation.

Corporate Governance

At Salam, we are committed to the highest standards of corporate governance, ensuring that every decision reflects transparency, accountability, and integrity. Our governance framework is designed to safeguard the interests of shareholders, promote stakeholder trust, and support sustainable long-term growth in alignment with Saudi regulatory requirements and international best practices.

Our Governance Framework

Salam’s corporate governance framework is founded on clear rules and standards that guide how the company is managed and controlled. Governed by its bylaws and Corporate Governance Manual, it defines the roles and responsibilities of shareholders, the Board, its committees, and senior management. Ongoing Board oversight, supported by internal policies and systems, ensures ethical conduct and regulatory compliance across the organization.

Board of Directors

The Board of Directors oversees Salam’s strategic direction, financial performance, and compliance with applicable laws and regulations. Comprising a mix of non-executive and independent members, the Board ensures objective judgment and balanced decision-making in the interest of all shareholders. Board responsibilities include:

  • Approving corporate strategy and key business policies.
  • Monitoring management performance and internal control systems.
  • Ensuring effective risk management and regulatory compliance.
  • Appointing and supervising senior executives.

Board Committees

To enhance efficiency and oversight, the Board has established the committees below, each operating under approved charters:

  • Audit Committee: Oversees internal and external audit functions, monitors financial integrity, and ensures robust compliance, internal control and risk management systems.
  • Nomination & Remuneration Committee: Oversees Board and executive management nomination processes and remuneration frameworks to ensure fairness, transparency, and alignment with company performance.
  • Executive Committee: Supports the Board in operational and strategic decision-making.
  • Additional committees may be established as needed to address specific business priorities.

Management and Internal Control

Salam’s executive management is entrusted with the day-to-day operation of the Company under clearly defined authority matrices. Salam also has an independent Internal Audit Department, reporting directly to the Audit Committee, which evaluates the effectiveness of governance, risk management, and compliance frameworks to ensure continuous improvement and accountability.

Ethics, Conduct, and Policies

Salam operates under a strong ethical foundation reinforced by a suite of internal policies, including:

  • Conflict of Interest and Business Ethics Policy
  • Stakeholder Protection Policy
  • Standards For Competing Business
  • Disclosure Policy
  • Whistleblowing Policy
  • Nomination Policy
  • Remuneration Policy
These policies ensure that ethical standards guide every action, protecting the company’s integrity and fostering trust among shareholders, customers, partners, and regulators.

Transparency and Disclosure

Transparency is at the heart of Salam’s governance philosophy. The company publishes regular reports on its financial and operational performance in line with regulatory requirements and best disclosure practices. Through its Investor Relations Policy, Salam ensures open and timely communication with shareholders and stakeholders.

Sustainability and Accountability

Salam integrates governance with sustainability principles, promoting responsible business conduct, data integrity, and social responsibility while contributing to national digital transformation objectives under Saudi Vision 2030.

Continuous Improvement

Our governance framework is continuously reviewed and updated to reflect evolving regulatory changes, technological advancements, and stakeholder expectations, ensuring that Salam remains a benchmark of good governance and corporate responsibility in the telecom sector.
